How I work

One playbook, from first insight to growth.

From physical products to AI agent automation, the job is the same: understand the customer, make the right call, build it well, launch it right and keep it growing.

  1. 01

    Listen

    Voice of the Customer research, review mining and market research to find gaps, complaints and unmet demand.

  2. 02

    Decide

    Build the business case on margin, total cost and projected sales, then set pricing and prioritize the roadmap.

  3. 03

    Build

    Write PRDs and work in Agile sprints with engineering, or negotiate with suppliers and review quality at the factory.

  4. 04

    Launch

    Lead go-to-market on Amazon, Target and Walmart with brand-aligned listings and creative, and shape go-to-market for AI products.

  5. 05

    Grow

    Track sales and margin, direct keyword and ad strategy, and flag reorders early so top sellers stay in stock.
